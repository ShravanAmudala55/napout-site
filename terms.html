What NapOut does

NapOut watches your trip using your phone's location and an estimated travel time, and rings an alarm as you get near the stop you chose.

What can stop it working

NapOut depends on your phone and on services it doesn't control. The alarm may ring late, early or not at all if, for example:

  • your battery runs out, or Low Power Mode limits the app;
  • location, alarm or other permissions are turned off or limited;
  • GPS or mobile signal is weak, such as underground or in tunnels;
  • the app is closed from the app switcher while a trip is armed;
  • your volume, Focus or other settings keep the alarm from being heard;
  • travel times, maps or stop locations are inaccurate or out of date.

Times shown in the app are estimates. When missing your stop would matter, set another alarm too or ask someone to wake you.
